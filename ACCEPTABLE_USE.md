# Acceptable Use

**This policy is a project statement, not legal advice or a guarantee of immunity.**

HRI is intended for analysis of software, devices, data, and systems that the user owns or is authorized to assess. Authorization must cover the target, methods, scope, and handling of collected information.

Do not use this project to:
- access systems or data without authorization;
- unlawfully circumvent technical protection measures or access controls;
- obtain, expose, or misuse credentials, private data, or confidential information;
- create or deploy malware, destructive payloads, or unauthorized persistence;
- evade security monitoring to conceal unauthorized activity;
- infringe intellectual property or violate contractual restrictions;
- violate privacy, export-control, sanctions, or other applicable laws.

A publicly reachable binary, firmware image, API, repository, or document is not automatically unrestricted for reverse engineering, redistribution, circumvention, or disclosure. Legal exceptions and contractual obligations depend on jurisdiction and facts. Users are responsible for obtaining permissions and legal advice.

Project maintainers may reject contributions or disable project functionality that creates unacceptable legal, privacy, or safety risks.
